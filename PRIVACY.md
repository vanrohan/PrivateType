# PrivateType privacy policy

Sonne Solutions, Ltd. publishes PrivateType.
This policy takes effect on January 19, 2027.

## Typing stays local

PrivateType runs prediction, spelling correction, language detection, and emoji search on your device.
PrivateType does not send your typing, usage data, analytics, or telemetry to servers.
The keyboard does not request Full Access.
PrivateType does not use an account, advertising service, or tracking service.

The app where you type still receives your text.
That app controls what happens to the text after insertion.
Apple controls system services, App Store services, and device backup services.
This policy describes PrivateType. It does not describe those other services.

## Local learning

Local learning is on by default unless you turn it off.
It stores bounded word counts, word pairs, and touch statistics on your device.
Word pairs can reveal parts of messages.
Use Local learning in keyboard settings to stop new personal learning.
Use Delete learned data to remove the saved learning and word protection choices.
Disabling learning does not delete existing saved data.

The keyboard can use text replacements and the supplementary lexicon that iOS provides.
Private typing pauses this personal lexicon use and automatic text replacements.

## Private typing

Private typing pauses personal word, touch, and emoji history.
It ends when you turn it off or close the keyboard.
Existing saved data stays present. Private typing does not delete it.
General word tables and explicit word protection choices still work.
The receiving app still receives text entered during private typing.

## Other local data

PrivateType stores your keyboard settings and explicit word protection choices.
Emoji history stores bounded counts and recent-use values on the device.
Use Clear in the emoji grid to remove emoji history.
The optional typing test stores test results and sample identifiers.
Use its delete control to remove saved results.
If you choose Export CSV, the file includes test metrics, device type, and app and system versions.
The CSV file contains no typed text. You choose where to share the file.

The in-app preview and installed keyboard keep separate settings and personal data.
Device backups can include stored PrivateType data under your backup settings.

## Contact and changes

Email support@sonnesolutions.com with privacy questions or requests.
Sonne Solutions, Ltd. updates this policy when the app's data practices change.
