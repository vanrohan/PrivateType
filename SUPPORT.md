# PrivateType support

Email support@sonnesolutions.com for help with PrivateType.
Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and the steps that show the problem.
Use a sample phrase when you report a typing problem. Keep personal text out of reports.

## Set up the keyboard

1. Open Settings, then General, then Keyboard.
2. Open Keyboards and tap Add New Keyboard.
3. Select PrivateType. Full Access is not required.
4. Open a text field. Hold the globe key and select PrivateType.

You can also tap Try the demo in the PrivateType app.
To change the installed keyboard settings, tap its gear above the letter keys.

## Manage local data

Open the gear and turn off Local learning to stop new learning.
Use Delete learned data to remove saved learning. Clear emoji history from the emoji grid.
Read the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) for details.
